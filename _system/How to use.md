# How to use your EMX vault

> [!tip] Stuck? Ask the vault
> Click the **book icon** on the far left (the Guide). Type a question like *how do I bookmark a tab?* and the answer shows up straight away, with a **Do it** button. No AI needed.

## Start here (5 minutes)

1. **Home** is your front page. It shows your **favorite projects**, **what needs you** and **mission control** (every project at a glance).
2. **The icons down the far left** (the ribbon) open everything: projects, tasks, recaps, decisions, the graph, the control panel and the Guide. Hover any icon to see its name.
3. **Every project has its own folder** under Games, Tools, Apps and so on. Its main note (the hub) has a **banner** at the top with buttons for that project.
4. **Your feedback goes in Feel tests.** Press **New playtest** on any project banner and write how it felt.
5. **Claude reads the vault too.** On a hub banner press **Ask Claude: Brief me** and paste it into Claude to catch it up.
6. **Backups:** turn on the automatic GitHub backup in Settings → EMX Vault Studio when you're ready.

## Moving around

| I want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Open any note | **Ctrl+O**, type part of its name |
| Search every note | **Ctrl+Shift+F** |
| Run any command | **Ctrl+P**, start typing what you want |
| Make a new note | **Ctrl+N** |
| Get back a closed tab | **Ctrl+Shift+T** |
| Open two notes side by side | Drag a tab to the right half of the screen, or right-click the tab → **Split right** |
| Keep a tab open | Right-click the tab → **Pin** |
| Hide or show the side panels | The panel icons at the very top left and top right |

## Bookmarks

- **Bookmark a note:** open it, then right-click its tab → **Bookmark…**. The same option is in the **⋯** menu at the note's top right, and when you right-click the note in the file list.
- **What can be bookmarked:** notes, folders, searches, headings and the graph. Panels like the Guide, Projects or Recap can't be, so right-clicking their tab won't show Bookmark. They're always one click away on the left ribbon instead.
- **Bookmark every open note at once:** Ctrl+P → *Bookmarks: Bookmark all tabs…*. They're saved as one group.
- **Open your bookmarks:** the bookmark icon at the very top of the left panel: the third one, after the folder (files) and the magnifier (search).
- **Bookmark a project:** Projects (folder icon) → the project's **⋯** menu → **Bookmark**.
- Bookmarks has no page in Settings. That's normal: it's already on.

## Your projects

- **See them all:** Home, or the **dashboard icon** (mission control). Each card shows the stage, open tasks, next step and your latest feedback.
- **Favorites:** press the **star** on a card. Your favorites from EMX Holo IDE show up by themselves.
- **On each favorite:** **Vault** opens the project's own dashboard, **Graph** opens its own graph, **Holo** opens it in Holo (Holo starts if it's closed).
- **Make a new project:** the **folder-plus icon** → pick what kind (Roblox game, website, design, video…) → name it → check the steps → **Create**. It's set up in the vault, Workshop and GitHub for you. Before anything happens it shows exactly what it will make.
- **Rename, move, archive or relate projects:** the **folder icon** (Projects) → the project's **⋯** menu. Links and Workshop follow. Nothing is ever deleted: archived projects go to Archive and can come back.

## Playtests and feedback

1. Open any note of the project.
2. Press **New playtest** on the banner. A dated entry appears at the top of Feel tests.
3. Write what felt right, what felt wrong, and any bugs.


## Tasks

- **Open:** the **checklist icon**, or **Tasks** on a project banner.
- **Add a task**, **Queue** it, or **Play** it (Claude starts working on it in a terminal).
- Finished tasks come back **For your review**. **Only you mark a task done**: press *Done (terminal)* and paste the command into your own terminal.
- The stage checklist is at the top. Tick items as you finish them.

## What did I do? (Recaps)

- **Open:** the **bar-chart icon**.
- Pick **Day, Week, Month** or any dates. You'll see sessions, commits, finished tasks, playtests and decisions, each compared with the period before.
- **Worth a look** points out things like blocked tasks or building without playtesting.
- Weekly recap notes are written by themselves into each project's **Recaps** folder.

## Decisions

- When you write something that clashes with a decision you made, it gets a **dotted orange underline**. Hover it to see why.
- It only warns you. It never changes your notes or decisions.
- **See all decisions:** the **gavel icon**. Mark each one *Still valid*, *Superseded* or *Outdated*.

## The graph

- **Open:** the **sparkles icon**. Every project is a branch and every note is a light.
- **Move it:** drag with the left mouse button. **Zoom:** the mouse wheel.
- **Turn it any way:** hold the **right mouse button** and drag. Left and right swing it round, up and down tip it. **Shift + right-drag** spins it flat.
- **Back to the start:** double-click.
- **Open a note:** click its light. **Ctrl+click** opens it beside the graph.
- **Full screen:** the square button in the corner. **Esc** leaves.
- **One project only:** press **Graph** on a project card or favorite.

### Graph Realms (looks for the graph)

- Click the **layers button** in the graph's corner, or go to control panel → **Graph** → **Graph Realms**.
- Pick one, like *Celestial World Tree*, *Dyson Ascendancy*, *Genesis Helix* or *Eternal Lotus*. The graph **morphs** into it.
- Realms only change the look. Your notes and links stay exactly the same.
- **Make your own:** change anything on the Graph tab (3D depth, camera tilt, turning by itself, colors, shape), then **Save as my realm**.
- **Undo realm** goes back to the look you had before.
- The **play button** in the corner tours every realm. Press it again to stop and get your look back.

## Making the vault look how you like

- **Open the control panel:** the **sliders icon**.
- **Looks:** click a card to try it. **Surprise me** picks a random one. **Undo** goes back.
- **Backgrounds:** **Import files…** or drop a video or shader on the drop zone. Click it, then choose where it shows: behind the vault, behind banners or behind the graph.
- **Projects:** each project's icon, color and banner background.

## Dashboards

- Home and every project have a dashboard. Press **Customize**, then **Add a widget**, pick one, then **Save**.
- Widgets include: favorites, what needs me, this week, tasks, playtests, decisions, commits, assets, trends and more.

## Assets and licenses

- **Projects → Assets** shows every image, sound, model and video in your projects.
- Set where each one came from (yours, CC0, licensed…). **Apply to every file in this folder** does a whole folder at once.
- Published Roblox games use only your own, CC0 or Roblox-licensed assets.

## Automations

- **Projects → Automations:** rules that run by themselves, like "every Monday, write the recap".
- Pick **when** (a new playtest, a day of the week…) and **what to do**. **Dry run** shows what it would do first, without doing it.

## Project types and blueprints

- Each kind of project (Roblox game, website, design…) is a **type**. It decides which notes a project gets, its checklist, its dashboard and its tools.
- **Projects → Types** edits them visually. Every change is saved as a new version, so you can always go back.
- **Export blueprint** saves a type (with its automations) to a file. **Import blueprint** brings one in, showing what would change first.

## Working with Claude

On a project's hub banner:

- **Ask Claude: Brief me** catches Claude up on the project.
- **Feel → tasks** turns your latest feedback into tasks.
- **Plan next** plans the next step.
- **Hand off** closes a long chat cleanly and gives you a prompt to start a fresh one.

Each button copies a short prompt. Paste it into Claude.

## Backups and getting things back

- **Automatic (optional):** turn on Backup in Settings → EMX Vault Studio once this folder is a git repo with a GitHub remote. It then commits and pushes every 10 minutes. The **cloud** at the bottom right says when.
- **An old version of a note:** right-click the note → **Note history** → pick a version → **Restore**.
- **A deleted note:** Ctrl+P → *Restore a deleted note*.
- Anything that looks like a password or key stops the backup and tells you where it is, so secrets never get uploaded.

## If something looks wrong

- **Ctrl+P → Reload app without saving.** Your notes are safe.
- A look went wrong? Control panel → **Undo**.
- The graph went odd? **Double-click** it, or **Undo realm**.
- Still stuck: the **Guide** (book icon) → search for it, or ask Claude.

## Where things are kept

- Your notes: plain Markdown files in this vault, readable by you and Claude.
- Settings, looks, realms and imported shaders: `.obsidian/plugins/emx-vault-studio/data.json`.
- Project types, categories, automations and asset licenses: notes in `_system/`.
- Your own Guide questions: `_system/Questions.md`.
- Imported videos: `_attachments/backgrounds/`.
